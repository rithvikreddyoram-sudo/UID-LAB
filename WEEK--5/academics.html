Academic Culture and System
Credit-Based Curriculum: Students follow a continuous evaluation system including assignments, seminars, and viva voce.
Value-Based Education: Inspired by founder Chancellor Sri Mata Amritanandamayi Devi (Amma), the curriculum emphasizes "Education for Life," integrating values and life skills like yoga and meditation into professional training.
Research Focus: The university maintains over 70 interdisciplinary Centers of Excellence and has established collaborations with 180+ global universities for research and student exchange.
Admissions: Entry into flagship programs like B.Tech is through the Amrita Engineering Entrance Exam (AEEE), while MBA admissions utilize CAT, MAT, and GMAT scores.
