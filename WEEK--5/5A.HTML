Nandamuri Taraka Rama Rao (born 20 May 1983), popularly known as NTR Jr, is an Indian actor, producer, and television presenter who primarily works in Telugu cinema.[1] He is one of the highest-paid actors in Indian cinema and has been featured in Forbes India's Celebrity 100 list since 2012.[2][3] Referred to in the media as the Man of Masses, he has starred in over 30 films.[4] He is a recipient of several accolades including, three Filmfare Awards South, three CineMAA Awards, a SIIMA Award, a IIFA Award and two Nandi Awards.
